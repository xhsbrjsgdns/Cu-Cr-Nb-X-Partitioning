Supplementary Material. Six sections document reference-space construction, descriptors and model protocols, chemical-identity diagnostics, Ni evaluation and recovery, literature consistency, and screening and confirmation.

Supplementary Data Dictionary. Population, precision, missing-value and field definitions for the thirty-one machine-readable data files.

Supplementary_Data_S1.csv. Reference-space counts by identity, sampling domain and temperature. 231 rows. New descriptive count; all failed rows retained.

Supplementary_Data_S2.csv. Observed raw phase instances and family mapping. 12 rows. Frozen phase inventory, including all observed Other solid types.

Supplementary_Data_S3.csv. Traceable phase-instance partition calculation. 12 rows. New worked example from one actual Zr state. Family amounts repeat per instance; do not sum the repeated family field.

Supplementary_Data_S4.csv. Complete candidate-feature dictionary, selection decisions and fold membership. 193 rows. Frozen decisions joined by exact feature name; no rerun of selection. Priority ranks reproduced from constant-list and pure name-key expressions only; no feature filtering rerun.

Supplementary_Data_S5.csv. Reference compositions and fixed data partitions. 50,000 rows. Frozen composition values and split assignments; X-free missing fields remain NA.

Supplementary_Data_S6.csv. IID task, family and temperature metrics. 137 rows. Union of frozen metric tables; NA denotes fields not applicable to a source row.

Supplementary_Data_S7.csv. Task-specific LOEO metrics. 516 rows. Distinct protocol from historical direct MLP and oracle.

Supplementary_Data_S8.csv. Complete-vector sums of squares and element-identity association. 8 rows. Source-backed existing full-precision recomputation, checked again for decomposition identity; no componentwise eta-squared averaging.

Supplementary_Data_S9.csv. Topology-conditioned counts and TVD error mass. 11 rows. Pooled share is sum of wrong-state TVD divided by sum of all-state TVD; macro is mean of six element shares.

Supplementary_Data_S10.csv. Reference-topology oracle results. 6 rows. Frozen affinity-augmented conditional regressor; not a routing-only ablation of the historical MLP.

Supplementary_Data_S11.csv. Ni compositions and frozen partition membership. 2,000 rows. All 2000 Ni composition rows retained.

Supplementary_Data_S12.csv. Three ordered Ni anchor sequences. 96 rows. Nested prefixes of 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 and 32 compositions; each composition supplies 11 states.

Supplementary_Data_S13.csv. All frozen random-sequence post-blind recovery results. 72 rows. Original per-sequence metrics; model roles retained.

Supplementary_Data_S14.csv. Post-blind recovery descriptive means and standard deviations. 24 rows. New descriptive summary; sample standard deviation ddof=1 over three anchor sequences, not a bootstrap interval.

Supplementary_Data_S15.csv. Recovery metrics stratified by reference topology. 288 rows. Frozen descriptive strata; no new experiment.

Supplementary_Data_S16.csv. Recovery metrics stratified by temperature regime. 288 rows. Frozen descriptive strata; no new experiment.

Supplementary_Data_S17.csv. All archived literature conditions. 149 rows. Frozen records retained; missing values remain NA; populations must not be conflated.

Supplementary_Data_S19.csv. Archived composition conversions. 149 rows. Frozen records retained; missing values remain NA; populations must not be conflated.
